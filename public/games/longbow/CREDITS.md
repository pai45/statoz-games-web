# Longbow credits

Longbow is an original archery game. Its rules, levels, rivals, interface, bow, arrows, targets, trees and other scenery
are original work for this project. The assets below come from CC0 (public domain) libraries. CC0 does not require
attribution, but we credit the authors with thanks.
