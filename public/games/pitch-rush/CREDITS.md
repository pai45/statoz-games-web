# Pitch Rush credits

Game: adapted from WebSoccer by Mark Clausing, MIT, pinned at
`e3628948208c4c89c781e86068d20320169f9fe5`. The original source and notice are
preserved in `vendor/websoccer`.

Artwork: [Sports Pack by Kenney Vleugels](https://kenney.nl/assets/sports-pack),
Creative Commons Zero (CC0). The original notice is `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
Selected originals are retained in `vendor/kenney-sports-pack`. Source hashes,
atlas rectangles, alpha bounds, pivots and keeper recolours are recorded in
`sports-art.json`.

Running, kick and slide poses are assembled from pack bodies and limb pieces.
Pitch markings follow the game's existing simulation geometry; the turf and
goal nets use pack tiles. Covers are original text-free compositions of the same
pack, reproduced by `scripts/import-pitch-rush-art.py`.
