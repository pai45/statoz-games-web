Audio & help

Bat first, then defend. A plays a ground shot; B plays an airborne shot. Hold the D-pad to place it. After contact, A starts a run and B reverses. On your bowling turn, select a D-pad direction first, then press A for a fast ball or B for a slow ball. With the ball, A throws to the keeper end and B to the bowler end.
