## Runtime behavior and adaptations

Transparent texture openings preserve cars and road below the beam; the existing HUD remains above the landmark. Finished study views hide it. Legacy arcade profiles retain their existing scenery. The gantry adds no collision box, lap trigger or checkpoint.

The source grid begins behind a distant arch, while the existing candidate simulation begins at the lap line. The initial approach is an explicitly designed presentation bridge: actual player travel maps to virtual projection distance. It stays still when the car is stopped or paused and clears only as the car advances. This warp follows the broad slow-then-fast source launch order but is not recovered world geometry or exact source timing.
