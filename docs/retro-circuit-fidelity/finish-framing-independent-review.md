Source lateral player drift, place typography/flash timing, scenery retention and exact native frame holds remain different or unmeasured.

**Verdict:** accept the scoped v10 correction for source-table ground/edge fitting, sparse-LA interpolation safety, pure monotonic departure and unchanged ordinary racing behavior. No additional automatic art correction is requested. This does not certify exact TopGear rendering, full-game parity, physical-phone performance or audible full-race acceptance. Those gates remain separate from this measured finish-framing improvement.
