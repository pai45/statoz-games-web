# Retro Hoops / six-athlete family v9

Build `character-v9-local`, assets `9.0.0`, rules `2.0.0`. Street Hoop is the confirmed reference. This local asset pass preserves the V8 basketball body construction, blue/red kits, full-viewport joystick controls, existing host title, account isolation, rules and AI. No push, pipeline activation or publication occurred.

The reference recording shows individual head/accessory silhouettes within each team. V8 used one repeated athlete per kit. V9 introduces six authored identities: blue dark visor, close crop and blond flat top; red cap, dark curls and blond swept hair. These are fictional counterparts, not source character names or extracted/traced commercial sprites. Research captures remain in the inspector and are excluded from runtime.

The editable generator creates 36 small head tiles: two teams, three identities, three views and two facings. The native 20x20 layers export uniformly to 32x32, using the same 1.6 scale as the existing body artwork. The runtime body atlas omits baked heads; Three and Canvas attach the selected identity to the pose's explicit head anchor. View comes from manifest metadata, including poses whose names contain `front` without describing the view. Blocking arms occlude the head; dynamic contact arms and the ball retain their existing order. Selection marker clearance accommodates taller caps/hair. Identity is stable by actor ID, including possession changes; no new save or simulation state is introduced.

All sixteen production simulation traces match V8 after excluding the new head-layer diagnostic metadata. The gallery preserves actual before/after scenes and supports team/player/view/facing selection, stepping, anchor overlays and all sixteen production action videos. V8 assets and its local review remain intact. Source files are snapshotted as text to avoid TypeScript discovering copies.
