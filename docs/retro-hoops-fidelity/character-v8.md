# Retro Hoops / basketball character foundation v8

Build `character-v8-local`, assets `8.0.0`, rules `2.0.0`. The user confirmed Street Hoop as the reference, rather than StatOz's separate Hoop Duel. The fullscreen joystick, blue/red teams, host title/ID, account isolation, AI and rules remain intact. Local only; no push, pipeline activation or publication.

The user found the incremental art revisions inadequate. This pass replaces the character construction rather than continuing to tune its palette alone. The source study identified upright ready poses, long necks, hands hanging beside the body, repeated limb construction, an oversized bright kit panel and overly fine artwork pixels as major differences. The original family now uses lower basketball ready stances, forward-carried hands, compact necks, loose shorts, broad blue/red cloth planes, folded running legs and distinct action postures. These are authored fictional athletes; no source sprites or traced contours enter the game.

The art is now drawn on a native 90x80 pixel grid and uniformly exported at 1.6x into the established 144x128 canvas/feet contract. This is an authored pixel-density fit, not proof of Street Hoop's native sprite grid. Anatomy is rebuilt from points; earlier rasters are not stretched to fix proportions. Near-arm rendering uses the same coarse grid, silhouette, sleeve and skin palette while its endpoint stays authoritative to the ball. The dunk gather moves the torso/supporting hand to meet the existing low/raised anchors. Its underlying ball-raise timing remains an identified source-fidelity gap. Selection arrows now follow each pose's actual head metadata in both renderers.

The venue receives a separate original illustration pass: seated and standing spectators with jacket, shirt, skin and cap variants, sparse cheering, more varied silhouettes and one exhibition desk/speaker assembly. Previous cloned desks and rectangular crowd bodies are replaced. Surface grain changes while legal court lines, baskets and simulation geometry remain fixed.

Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_k8IIS9n3dk . The retained first-possession overview and frame 0067 support the visual observations. Reference images stay in the research viewer and are excluded from executable assets. The comparison displays source/production at equal image height but explicitly does not claim matched pose, depth or camera alignment. `reference-comparison.json` records original-art bounds/anchors, source provenance and the remaining uncertainties.

The actual Three renderer is captured before/after at the same seeded diagnostic moments. All sixteen scenario simulation traces match V7, isolating the art change from gameplay. The inspector supports both teams, front/side/back, mirrored facing, pose stepping, joint markers and sixteen complete production loops. Both V7 and the fullscreen-controls candidate remain preserved; previous source files are snapshotted as text in this review directory.

Validation: 86 tests, nine asset integrity/ground checks, sixteen rendered and decoded action loops, twelve mobile/control lifecycle checks, six team scenarios, twelve gallery combinations, six exported host-flow checks, lint, typecheck and static production build passed. Source/pose sheets, actual production dribble and dunk gather frames, pass/dunk contact sheets and mobile captures were visually inspected. These are integrity/playability checks, not an exact-art approval.

Exact Street Hoop likeness remains incomplete. Full source-specific action silhouettes, native cadence, ball-raise transitions, matched camera/depth comparisons and individual athlete variation remain open. The crowd is original stylised scenery. Physical-device performance remains untested. Source-likeness approval stays false; a sprite count or passing build does not establish 1:1 parity.
